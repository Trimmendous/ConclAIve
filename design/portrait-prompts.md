## Shared prompt

> Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: production character portrait expression sheet for a dark desktop app. Create one original fictional character, **{name}**, **{role}**. Show exactly three equal vertical panels left-to-right: stoic, talking, angry. Subject: **{appearance}**. Entire body visible head to toe, standing straight, arms relaxed at sides, facing camera. Premium stylized 3D editorial character art with clean shapes, refined textures, and a slightly graphic finish; original non-celebrity design. Wide 1536×1024 horizontal triptych with three equal panels; character centered at identical size and exact same rigid pose in every panel; generous margin above the head and below the shoes; straight-on camera. Soft cinematic studio light on a deep charcoal-to-slate gradient background with restrained **{color}** rim light. Identity, face structure, hair, outfit, body, pose, hands, camera, crop, background, shadows, and lighting must be identical across all panels. Only the facial expression changes. Left: closed mouth and neutral brows. Middle: mouth naturally open mid-syllable and engaged brows. Right: closed tense mouth, sharply furrowed brows, and glaring eyes. No gestures. Avoid text, labels, borders, dividers, props, logos, watermarks, extra people, cropped feet, and real-person or celebrity likenesses.
